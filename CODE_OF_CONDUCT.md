# Code of Conduct / 행동 강령

This is a short version based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(version 2.1, licensed under CC BY 4.0).
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/ko/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 2.1을 바탕으로 줄여 쓴 버전이에요.

## Our pledge / 약속

We want GAMJA to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their age, background, experience, gender,
language, nationality or ability. We pledge to act in ways that help an open, welcoming, diverse and healthy community.

## What we expect / 이렇게 해 주세요

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Not everyone has the same experience or the same first language.
- Respect different opinions and give constructive feedback. Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## What is not acceptable / 이런 건 안 돼요

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or discriminatory language
- Sexualized language or images, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing other people's private information (addresses, accounts, conversations) without permission
- Trolling, spam, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and enforcement / 적용 범위와 조치

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other space of the project, and when someone
represents the project in public.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and temporarily or permanently ban people
whose behavior breaks these rules. To report a problem, write to **help@webgamja.com**. Reports are kept
confidential, and we will treat every report fairly.
