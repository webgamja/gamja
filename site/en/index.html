FAQ

More questions? Write to help@webgamja.com.

Is it really free?

GAMJA is open source and free to use. It uses Claude, which needs your own Claude subscription from Anthropic, and you pay Anthropic for that. GAMJA is an independent project, not affiliated with Anthropic, and Claude is a trademark of Anthropic.

Where are my chats and files stored?

On your phone. GAMJA does not collect your conversations or send them to its own servers.

Which phones work?

Android 8 or newer with plenty of free storage. Keep it on Wi-Fi and plugged in during the first setup.

Why is it not on Google Play?

GAMJA runs a small Linux environment inside your phone. Android 10 and newer block that for apps built to the latest targets, so GAMJA is distributed directly from GitHub.
