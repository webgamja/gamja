FAQ

More questions? Write to help@webgamja.com.

Is it really free?

GAMJA is open source and free to use. It uses Claude, which needs your own Claude subscription from Anthropic, and you pay Anthropic for that. GAMJA does not collect or resell it.

Where are my conversations and files stored?

On your phone. GAMJA does not collect your conversations or send them to its own servers. Your messages go to Claude (Anthropic), so please also read Anthropic's privacy policy. This website may use Google Analytics for visit statistics, and only counts you if you agree (see the privacy policy).

Which phones work?

Android 8 or newer with plenty of free storage (a Linux environment and tools are installed on the phone, which needs a few GB). Keep it on Wi-Fi and plugged in during the first 10 to 20 minutes. To keep it running, set GAMJA's battery usage to "Unrestricted".

Why is it not on Google Play?

GAMJA runs a small Linux environment and programs inside your phone. Android 10 and newer block that for apps built to the latest targets, which does not fit Google Play's requirements. So GAMJA is distributed directly from GitHub. A lighter app may come later.

Is it safe?

All of the code is public, so anyone can check it. GAMJA is a tool that lets whoever signs in read and change files on that phone, so please choose a long password and use it only on the same Wi-Fi or over a VPN. Report security problems privately through GitHub or at help@webgamja.com.
