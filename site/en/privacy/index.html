Privacy policy

Effective: November 2, 2026. The Korean version is the original; this English text is provided for convenience.

GAMJA takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what we do with information on the website (webgamja.com) and in email inquiries, and how the GAMJA app handles your data.

1. In short

  • In the GAMJA app, your conversations and files are stored only on your own phone. The GAMJA operator does not collect or see them.
  • On the website, visit statistics (Google Analytics) are counted only if you agree. Before you agree, nothing is loaded.
  • If you email us, we receive your address and message in order to reply.

2. What we collect and how

CategoryDataHow
Visit statistics (only if you agree)A random cookie identifier, pages viewed and time, referrer, browser, device and screen type, language, approximate location (country/city level)Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC)

We configure Google Analytics not to store IP addresses and not to use advertising data (advertising identifiers, Google signals).

3. Why we use it

  • Visit statistics: to see what is helpful and improve the website.
  • Inquiries: to answer you and keep a record.
  • Access logs: to deliver the website securely and prevent abuse (handled by the host).

4. Retention

  • Visit statistics: we set Google Analytics data retention to 14 months; it is deleted automatically afterwards.
  • Email inquiries: kept only as long as needed to handle them (at most one year).
  • Access logs: according to the hosting provider's policy.

5. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your information or pass it on for other purposes. To run the website, the following providers process data.

If you do not want a transfer to Google, simply do not agree to visit statistics. (Access logs are needed to open the page at all.)

6. Cookies and withdrawing consent

  • If you agree to visit statistics, Google Analytics stores cookies. If you do not agree, it does not.
  • You can change your choice at any time with "Visit statistics" at the bottom of each page, or delete cookies in your browser.
  • Your light/dark choice is stored only in your own browser and is not sent anywhere.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your information. Write to help@webgamja.com and we will respond without undue delay after confirming it is you. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA or the UK), you may have additional rights, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

8. The GAMJA app and your data

  • Where data lives: conversations, files and password settings stay on your phone. They are not sent to the GAMJA operator.
  • Claude (Anthropic): GAMJA runs Claude Code with your own Claude account. You sign in on Anthropic's official screen and GAMJA never sees your login. Your messages are sent through Claude to Anthropic and are governed by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.
  • Update checks: to look for new versions, the app connects to GitHub (our public release repository). GitHub can see your IP address. GAMJA sends no conversation content or device identifiers.
  • GitHub connection (optional): if you turn it on, files made by your crews are recorded in your own private GitHub repository. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Tools you install in the Toolbox and outside services used by crews follow their own providers' policies.

9. Children

GAMJA and the website are not directed at children under 14. We do not knowingly collect information from them and delete it if we learn of it.

10. Security

We limit who can access your information, and protect the website connection with HTTPS. The app limits password attempts and keeps your conversations and files on your phone. The code is public, so anyone can review it.
